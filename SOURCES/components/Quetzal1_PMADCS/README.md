# Quetzal-1 passive ADCS flight reference

The locally retained 2023 Journal of Small Satellites paper documents the exact
K&J `D84` magnet, two 95 mm × 1 mm HyMu 80 rods, their placement and on-orbit
performance in a 1U imaging CubeSat. The paper is copyrighted and excluded from
Git; the official URL and local hash are preserved in the manifest.

The open Quetzal-1 hardware repository was inspected at commit
`d4d1b59de384701a016a6e17353aff3c8ba64853`. It is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 and is
referenced directly rather than copied into this repository:
https://github.com/Quetzal-1-CubeSat-Team/quetzal1-hardware
