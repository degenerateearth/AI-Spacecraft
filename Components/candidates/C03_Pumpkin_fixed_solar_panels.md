Status: **CANDIDATE / EVIDENCE INCOMPLETE — NOT PHASE 1 BASELINE**  
Audit date: 2026-09-15

## Publicly established

- Manufacturer: Pumpkin, Inc.
- Current family page: CubeSat Kit Fixed Solar Panels.
- Public family price range: USD 2,500–5,650, recorded without currency conversion.
- Substrate thickness: 0.031 in / 0.8 mm.
- Manufacturer states every panel receives vacuum bakeout and irradiance testing.
- Manufacturer states current triple-junction cells exceed 32% AM0 efficiency.
- A separate USD 590 eight-clip set is described for mounting up to six panels.

## Why it does not pass

- The store selector is marked `Unavailable`.
- Exact 1U face part numbers/configurations are not exposed by the current page.
- Exact planforms, masses, mounting patterns and camera/antenna accommodations are
  absent.
- Cell identity, stringing, BOL hot/cold IV limits, EOL degradation and tolerances
  are absent.
- Harness, connector and pinout data are absent.
- Operating, storage, radiation and configuration-specific qualification limits
  are absent.

The family therefore cannot close the Degen-1 orbit/attitude power model, face
layout, mass budget, harness, thermal limits or twelve-month degradation case.
