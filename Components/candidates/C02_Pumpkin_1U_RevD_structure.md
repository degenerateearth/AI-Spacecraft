## Why it does not pass

- Current product selectors are marked `Unavailable`; current commercial
  obtainability of the exact set is therefore not established.
- The exact base/cover/switch combination compatible with Degen-1's launch-inhibit
  concept and AntS location is not selected.
- Exact current set mass and part-level controlled drawings are not established.
- Torque, locking method and configuration-specific qualification/acceptance
  evidence are not public.
- The public CAD license supports integration modeling but restricts manufacturing
  and quotation use; it is not released fabrication data.

## Analyses blocked

Phase 1 admission, released mass properties, detailed load path, exact inhibit
geometry, procurement release and configuration-specific launch-survival claim.
