## Architecture consequences requiring later implementation

1. SAM-S is a proposed antenna replacement only. Its added detail justifies replacing the earlier candidate; its greater indicated price is a documented tradeoff, not a purchase commitment. The +Z panel, coax, command software, inhibition and deployment geometry all require reconciliation.
2. Do not substitute Amun, Eddie or Murgas without revising their carrier, power and software interfaces. No evidence supports cross-vendor stacking merely because connectors look similar or buses have the same name.
3. Ground receive gain requires a protected transmit bypass and control interlock. A transmit event routed into the selected LNA can exceed its input limit. Filter, switching, sequencing and enclosure remain a custom integration task.
4. A mast that is mechanically strong can still violate the M2 antenna's electrical installation requirements. The specified nonconductive support is a real missing design item, not an optional accessory.
