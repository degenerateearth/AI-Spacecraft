# D1-STR-001 — 1U rail frame and load structure

Revision A · 2026-09-16  
State: **CUSTOM/TO BE VERIFIED — nominal geometry and material selected; manufacturing release incomplete**

## Controlled selection

Degen-1 uses a custom 1U machined-aluminum rail frame derived from the
MIT-licensed BIRDS-4 flight-model geometry at upstream commit
`3bab47a0f53410ef79005d21321ff659726f0a1c`. The nominal Revision-A frame
geometry is the 22 top-level `B4_frame*` / `B4_EM_frame*` instances in
`BIRDS4_FM_Final v7.step`. External solar panels, the antenna end panel,
camera aperture panel, launch switches, internal boards and their mounting
hardware are separate Degen-1 assemblies.

This selection adopts known geometry as a reference and manufacturing starting
point. It does not purchase a BIRDS structure, assign BIRDS qualification to
Degen-1, or assert that the upstream STEP set is a complete drawing package.

## Nominal geometry extracted with FreeCAD

`drawings/freecad/structure_reference/inspect_birds4_reference.py` imports the
pinned full assembly, selects direct-child frame objects, and generates
`reference_geometry.json`. FreeCAD 1.1.3 produced:

| Quantity | Value | Evidence class |
|---|---:|---|
| Top-level frame instances | 22 | MODELED extraction from pinned upstream STEP |
| Combined nominal frame envelope X | 100.000004 mm | MODELED; rounding/noise in STEP coordinates |
| Combined nominal frame envelope Y | 100.000004 mm | MODELED; rounding/noise in STEP coordinates |
| Combined nominal frame envelope Z | 113.500000 mm | MODELED; rounding/noise in STEP coordinates |
| Combined solid volume | 56,430.596 mm³ | MODELED/CALCULATED |
| Mass if every selected solid were 6061 at 2.70 g/cm³ | 152.363 g | CALCULATED ASSUMPTION; not an as-built mass |
| Center of volume | recorded in `reference_geometry.json` | MODELED; not spacecraft center of mass |

The nominal bounds are approximately X `-49.9997` to `+50.0003` mm, Y
`-50.0002` to `+49.9998` mm and Z `-56.7501` to `+56.7499` mm in the upstream
assembly coordinate system. These match the public EXOpod Nova nominal 1U rail
span and length values, 100.0 × 100.0 × 113.5 mm. A nominal match is not a
tolerance or fit result.

The 152.363 g value deliberately remains conditional. The grouped upstream
`frame1F` and `frame3` parts contain multiple solids, including hardware-like
objects, and the STEP files do not control every material. Degen-1 shall replace
the conditional mass with a released part-material table and then with measured
as-built mass if hardware is fabricated.

## Degen-1 material and finish decisions

The following are Degen-1 project decisions for the custom derivative:

1. Machine all frame and rail bodies from certified **6061-T6 aluminum** stock.
   Plate/sheet procurement shall invoke ASTM B209 where applicable; extruded or
   bar stock shall invoke ASTM B221. Mill certificates must identify alloy,
   temper and lot.
2. Apply a controlled hard-anodized finish to the four deployer-contact rail
   surfaces. The released finish note shall invoke the current applicable
   revision of MIL-PRF-8625 Type III, Class 1 unless the mission ICD requires a
   different coating. Coating build-up is part of the final rail tolerance.
3. Use a documented non-hexavalent chemical-conversion finish on non-rail
   conductive/bonding surfaces where needed, with masked electrical-bonding
   lands defined on the drawings. The final process specification and class
   remain open until the bonding/thermal design is released.
4. No coating, adhesive, lubricant or polymer is accepted solely because it is
   present in an upstream model. Every added material belongs in the Degen-1
   materials/outgassing register.
5. Fasteners, inserts, locking method, torque, electrical bonding and galvanic
   isolation are controlled by D1-MECH-HW-001, not implied by the STEP solids.

## Mechanical interface control

- Nominal rail span: 100.0 mm in X and Y.
- Nominal rail length: 113.5 mm in Z.
- Public EXOpod provisional tolerances: 100.0 ±0.1 mm rail span and
  113.5 ±0.5 mm rail length.
- Maximum public EXOpod space between rails: 87.2 mm.
- Degen-1 shall not consume the public 25 mm protrusion allowance as a design
  entitlement; deployed and mission-specific allowances require the final ICD.
- The four rail contact faces are primary datums. The final drawing set must
  control flatness, parallelism, coplanarity, perpendicularity, surface
  roughness, corner radii and coating thickness against those datums.
- Solar-panel PCBs and end panels must mount inside the rail-controlled
  envelope with positive clearance under full tolerance and launch deflection.
- At least three physically independent launch-switch actuators and accessible
  RBF/service features must be integrated without weakening a rail or creating
  a deployer-contact protrusion. Their locations remain D1-FAB-001 work.
- Internal board and battery mounts must transfer launch load into multiple
  frame members and remain serviceable with a defined assembly order.

The BIRDS-4 full assembly reaches about 104 × 108.3 × 114.5 mm because it
includes panels, switches and other hardware. That full envelope is not adopted.
Only the extracted frame set produces the nominal 100 × 100 × 113.5 mm rail
envelope. Degen-1 external assemblies must be independently checked.

## Fabrication release required

The upstream STEP geometry is not sufficient for fabrication release. Degen-1
must produce:

1. one controlled native FreeCAD body and dimensioned drawing for every unique
   frame part, with Degen-1 part number and upstream-derivative attribution;
2. an assembly drawing with quantity, orientation, datum and fastener tables;
3. explicit general and critical tolerances, geometric tolerances, edge breaks,
   thread/inserts, surface roughness, coating masks and post-finish dimensions;
4. an exact stock/material/finish BOM and supplier process requirements;
5. a manufacturing inspection plan and measurement uncertainty for every
   deployer-controlled feature;
6. interface drawings for side panels, end panels, backplane, battery,
   avionics stack, camera, antenna mechanism, ADCS retainers and harness clips;
7. a released assembly sequence with tool access and torque/locking controls;
   and
8. a mass-properties table derived from the released parts rather than the
   all-6061 reference assumption.

## Analysis and verification path

`D1-STR-001` remains `CUSTOM/TO BE VERIFIED` until the project has:

1. a tolerance stack showing compliance with the provisional public envelope;
2. static strength, buckling, fastener, insert and contact-bearing calculations
   using the selected launch environment and conservative allowables;
3. a modal model with mesh and boundary-condition convergence evidence;
4. coupled load inputs or conservative mission-specific qualification levels;
5. panel/rail, switch, RBF, antenna-sweep, camera-aperture and cable-clearance
   checks in the complete assembly;
6. a virtual deployer fit check against the controlled model and, before flight,
   physical fit-check evidence in the required deployer or test pod;
7. dimensional inspection and mass/center-of-mass measurement on any fabricated
   unit;
8. vibration and shock verification followed by inspection, continuity,
   deployment and functional checks; and
9. independent mechanical review before a flight release.

The theoretical design may complete the drawings and reproducible analyses
without fabricating hardware. It may not claim qualified strength, deployer
compatibility or launch survival without the later physical and mission-specific
evidence.
