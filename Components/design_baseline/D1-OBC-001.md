# D1-OBC-001 — BIRDS-derived command and data handling board

Revision A · 2026-09-16  
State: **CUSTOM/TO BE VERIFIED — architecture and core parts selected; Degen-1 schematic/layout not released**

## Function

D1-OBC-001 will provide boot and safe-state control, command parsing, health
telemetry collection, payload sequencing, radio control, watchdog/reset,
nonvolatile image storage and the ground-service/programming interface.

## Storage allocation

One 1 Gbit flash provides 128,000 kB decimal capacity in the BIRDS ICD and is
sufficient for occasional compressed images at the mission level. Degen-1 will
retain at least two physically separate storage devices if the mass/power/layout
budget closes: one for mission data and one for logs/configuration. The BIRDS BOM
contains four devices; Degen-1 quantity is not frozen until the image format,
wear strategy and fault-containment design are released.

No image capacity claim is made until an exact camera output format and maximum
file size are measured or manufacturer-specified.

## Deliberate deviations from BIRDS-4

- The unavailable BIRDS PCB layout will not be reconstructed by visual guess.
  Degen-1 will issue a new KiCad schematic and layout from the public circuit
  record and a controlled interface specification.
- The obsolete `CX5032GA08000H0PST02` crystal will not be copied. An exact current
  oscillator/crystal will be selected and its load network recalculated.
- Empty or ambiguous BOM lines, including the 560 pF capacitor flagged by the
  BIRDS BOM, require exact replacement parts.
- Command parsing will use fixed message definitions, length bounds, replay
  protection and authentication appropriate to the eventual FCC service. Dynamic
  evaluation or execution of received text is prohibited.

## Evidence limits

The selected Microchip parts are commercial microcontrollers, not radiation
hardened devices. BIRDS use and related flight history motivate the architecture
but do not establish Degen-1 radiation tolerance. TID/SEE analysis, fault-rate
assumptions, watchdog recovery and representative irradiation or expert review
remain open. No D1-OBC-001 hardware exists.
