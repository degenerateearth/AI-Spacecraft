# D1-CAM-001 - Earth-imaging payload

Revision A - 2026-09-16  
State: **SELECTED FOR DETAILED DESIGN - exact camera and functional interface selected; carrier/harness not released**

## Exact selection and mission role

Degen-1 selects the SkyFox Labs **`piCAM/FM`**, default approximately 60-degree
diagonal field-of-view optics, IR-cut filter and infinity focus. The `/FM`
configuration is required; `/EM` is not interchangeable. The payload produces a
640 x 480 RGB JPEG for the mission objective of returning an occasional
recognizable Earth image.

The manufacturer datasheet includes image examples from a 530 km sun-synchronous
orbit. Those examples establish that this camera type has produced recognizable
orbital imagery. They do not establish Degen-1 pointing probability, exposure,
thermal survival, radiation life or mission success.

## Six-pin interface

| Pin | Name | Direction/use | Degen-1 connection |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | `VDD` | 3.3 V input | protected `+3V3_B_CAM` branch |
| 2 | `ON` | high keeps internal power switch on | OBC-controlled, default low, with defined pulldown |
| 3 | `RXD` | UART input to camera | OBC UART TX through a tri-state-capable interface |
| 4 | `TXD` | UART output from camera | OBC UART RX through a disconnect/low-hold interface |
| 5 | `FLASH` | open-collector output, 20 mA maximum | no-connect in the flight baseline; no external flash is fitted |
| 6 | `GND` | power/case ground | payload ground and conductive mounting reference |

The camera datasheet names the installed connector only as the Molex 53261
series. It does not identify the exact suffix or contact finish. The harness
housing is selected as Molex `510210600`; the terminal suffix remains a mandatory
as-configured reconciliation because the correct mating finish must match the
camera header. Candidate manufacturer terminals are not promoted to the baseline
until that header configuration is known.

## Power and latch-up containment

The camera branch uses TI **`TPS2553DRVR-1`**, an active 2.5-6.5 V adjustable
current-limited load switch with latch-off response and reverse-current blocking,
in the 2 x 2 mm WSON package. The part is located on D1-EPS-001 and feeds only
the camera.

The exact `R_ILIM` is not frozen in this record. It shall be calculated from the
TI datasheet equations and tolerances after camera startup current is measured or
conservatively bounded. The limit must exceed valid startup/capture current and
remain below the branch, connector and converter fault limits. No resistance or
maximum camera power is invented from the typical 95 mA value.

The required power-cycle sequence is:

1. drive `ON`, camera `RXD`, and the OBC-side UART idle driver low or high
   impedance for at least one second;
2. enable the protected 3.3 V branch while preserving those states;
3. assert `ON` high; and
4. enable the normal UART idle-high state and require the documented boot text.

This sequence prevents UART back-powering described by the manufacturer.

## Command and data definition

The flight software shall implement the manufacturer one-byte commands directly.
Baseline capture uses ASCII `d` for daylight capture without flash. Commands
`D`, `m`, `M`, `n`, `N`, `s`, `S`, `t`, `T`, and bitrate selections `0` through
`9` remain available for ground verification but are not autonomous mission
defaults.

Each image is emitted as fixed 67-byte ASCII records:

- byte 0: `@`;
- bytes 1-4: four ASCII hexadecimal digits for the record number;
- bytes 5-8: four ASCII hexadecimal digits for the total-record field;
- bytes 9-64: 28 JPEG bytes represented by 56 ASCII hexadecimal characters;
- bytes 65-66: CR/LF.

A final `FACE` telemetry record reports the JPEG raw-byte count and camera health
values. Degen-1 shall store the received records and reconstructed binary JPEG,
reject malformed hex/length fields, detect missing/duplicate record numbers, and
never assume the datasheet's 27,719-byte example is a maximum image size.

## Required custom carrier and mechanical work

The D1-CAM-001 carrier is part of the custom payload assembly and must release:

1. a bracket drawing using the manufacturer STEP model and four M2.5 fasteners;
2. an aperture drawing with at least 1 mm radial clearance to the selected lens;
3. exact fastener material, locking method, conductive bonding and torque;
4. a six-conductor harness drawing with the reconciled PicoBlade contact;
5. UART isolation/low-hold circuitry and reset-state truth table;
6. optical cleanliness, lens-cap removal and focus inspection instructions; and
7. fit, boot, capture, packet-reconstruction, current-limit and thermal-vacuum
   verification procedures.

a guaranteed maximum operating/startup current, radiation assurance, full
environmental qualification report, selected-lens outer envelope, or exact
connector suffix. Those omissions prevent a tested/qualified claim. The first
three are addressed by branch protection and later measurement/qualification;
the lens STEP and connector configuration must be reconciled before final
mechanical and harness release.
