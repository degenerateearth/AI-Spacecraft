# D1-HAR-001 — flight and service harness baseline

Revision A · 2026-09-17  
State: **CUSTOM/TO BE VERIFIED — complete harness topology and principal COTS materials selected; CAD-routed lengths, panel terminations and camera contact finish remain open**

## Scope and completeness result

This record accounts for every wire or cable that crosses between presently
selected Degen-1 assemblies. The five plug-in electronics boards communicate
through D1-BPB-001's fixed 50-pin connectors; separate board-to-board signal
cables are therefore prohibited. D1-ADCS-001 is passive and has no electrical
harness. The two RBF jacks are mounted directly on D1-FAB-001 and have no
flight pigtails.

The remaining flight harness set is one battery loom, five panel looms, three
independent deployer-switch leads, one camera cable and one RF coax. One
removable service cable is ground support equipment and does not fly. The
machine-readable schedule is `harness_schedule.csv`.

The JST PA family rating is 3 A at AWG 22 and -40 to +105 °C. Those catalog
limits are not adopted as spacecraft current or thermal qualification. Degen-1

use contact resistance, wire resistance, temperature and vacuum derating rather
than the headline family rating.

Use one unmodified Hirose `UFL-2LPA-068Y2T-A-100`. Both PCB ends use

No conductor supplies spacecraft power. This cable is removed before flight.

flight-harness mass is claimed because the final wire lengths, camera contact,

## Assembly and verification requirements

1. Produce a drawing for each serialized harness with end views, cavity numbers,
   net names, wire part/color/gauge, cut and strip lengths, tolerances, twist
   instructions, branch points, lacing, labels and allowable repair method.
2. Fabricate to NASA-STD-8739.4A principles using trained personnel and the
   connector manufacturer's controlled crimp tooling/process. This project has
   not certified any assembler to that standard.
3. Use no inline flight splice, solder-tinned crimp conductor, consumer PVC
   wire, adhesive cable tie mount, or nylon zip tie. Any necessary splice opens
   this record and requires an exact qualified splice system.
4. Key, label or physically separate adjacent connectors. Identical solar-panel
   connectors are acceptable only because their pin maps are identical; face
   identity is carried by harness ID and EPS channel label.
5. Provide independent strain relief before every PCB termination. Harness
   restraint clips and fasteners are D1-MECH-HW-001 items; solder joints and
   push-on U.FL connectors do not carry harness inertia.
6. Inspect 100% for part identity, strip/crimp dimensions, insulation support,
   contact seating, pin assignment, workmanship, length and damage.
7. Test 100% continuity and isolation before and after installation; record
   four-wire resistance for each power conductor and the complete RF cable's
   insertion/return loss over the released band.
8. Repeat continuity/isolation after vibration and thermal-vacuum testing.
9. Close voltage drop and wire/contact temperature at worst-case current and
   temperature before release. The present wire voltage rating is irrelevant to
   this low-voltage current/thermal calculation.
10. Control connector mating counts; the U.FL and PicoBlade public records each
    specify 30 cycles, which is not a target integration count.
