## Calculated pack quantities

- Nominal rated energy: `3.6 V × 2.4 Ah = 8.64 Wh`.
- Six-cell bare mass: `6 × 24 g = 144 g`.
- Maximum physical cell envelope using stated positive tolerances is not closed:
  the diameter has no positive tolerance, while height can reach 52.0 mm.
- Interconnect, insulation, sensor, restraint, enclosure and harness mass are
  unknown until D1-BAT-001 drawings are released; they are not treated as zero.

These calculations do not establish usable orbital energy. Capacity versus
temperature, charge efficiency, aging, self-discharge and converter losses must
be applied in the power budget from manufacturer curves or test data.

## Selection rationale

The BIRDS platform demonstrates a six-cell 3S2P NiMH architecture and publishes
cell-screening, pack-assembly and safety procedures. Its historical HR-3UPT
Eneloop cell is not silently reused. `BK120AAHU` is a current, identifiable
industrial long-life AA cell with manufacturer data and wider specified charge
and discharge temperature ranges than the historical BIRDS cell.

The lower capacity compared with the 2,000 mAh BIRDS cell reduces nominal pack
energy from 14.4 Wh to 8.64 Wh. This is a deliberate design change. The power
and eclipse budgets must close with the new value before the cell can be
SUPPORTED.

## Required custom-pack release artifacts

1. Cell orientation, series/parallel interconnect and welded-tab drawing.
2. Exact nickel strip or bus material, insulation, wire, connector and fuse BOM.
3. Double-fault isolation and external-short protection schematic.
4. Temperature-sensor part and placement drawing.
5. Cell restraint/enclosure drawing with vent paths and no rail interference.
6. Charge algorithm and termination limits derived from the Panasonic data and
   reviewed against the D1-EPS-001 converter/protection circuit.
7. Lot-controlled cell-screening and matching procedure.
8. Pack acceptance procedure covering polarity, OCV, capacity, impedance,
   isolation, continuity, thermal sensors and inhibit behavior.

## Verification path

The BIRDS cell-screening method is adopted as a reference, not as Degen-1 test
evidence: characterize each cell; expose candidate cells to vacuum and vibration;
repeat mass, OCV, capacity and impedance measurements; assemble matched cells;
then repeat pack electrical and environmental verification. Numerical acceptance
limits will be released after the manufacturer curves, launch environment and
measurement uncertainty are reconciled.

Battery safety review, launch-provider acceptance and physical testing remain
downstream. IEC 62133-1 certification does not establish vacuum or launch
compatibility.
