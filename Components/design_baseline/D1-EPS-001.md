# D1-EPS-001 — electrical power and launch-inhibit board

Revision A · 2026-09-16  
State: **CUSTOM/TO BE VERIFIED — topology and principal parts selected; schematic/layout not released**

## Function

D1-EPS-001 collects power from body-mounted solar panels, charges the 3S2P
NiMH battery, enforces cold-launch isolation, creates the regulated spacecraft
rails, switches high-load branches and measures power-system health. It is a new
Degen-1 design derived from the public BIRDS safety architecture. BIRDS flight
history does not transfer to this board.

## Frozen electrical architecture

```text
five panel inputs -> per-panel blocking/protection -> common solar raw bus
                  -> LTC3119 solar buck-boost front end (6.0 V charger bus)
                  -> BQ25172 3-cell NiMH charger -> D1-BAT-001 (3S2P)

D1-BAT-001 -> RBF battery isolator -> three independent deployer inhibits
           -> protected battery bus
           -> two independent TPS63020-Q1 3.3 V rails
           -> two TPS2553-1 protected/switched 3.3 V payload branches
           -> two separately protected/switched unregulated branches
```

Ground return isolation, inhibit placement and the external-charge path will
follow the fault-containment intent in BIRDS Platform ICD Rev F §7.2–7.3. The
final schematic must show that a single open/short failure cannot bypass the
required inhibited state. This statement is a design requirement, not a completed
fault-tree result.

## Charger configuration release values

The BQ25172 is configured for the three **series groups** in D1-BAT-001. Parallel
cells do not change the charger cell-count setting.

| Pin | Revision-A value | Derived behavior | Evidence/calculation |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `VSET` | 18.0 kΩ ±1% | 3-cell intermittent charge | TI datasheet Table 7-2 |
| `ISET` | 1.24 kΩ ±1% | nominal `300 AΩ / 1,240 Ω = 0.242 A` total pack charge current | TI equation; approximately 0.101 C for a 2.4 Ah pack |
| `TMR` | 8.20 kΩ ±1% | 16-hour full safety timer; 3.2-hour intermittent cycle | TI datasheet Table 7-1 and 20% intermittent behavior |
| `TS` | `103AT-2` on the center cell pair | hardware charge permission approximately 0–45 °C in the nominal TI network | TI datasheet; exact thresholds and resistor network must be checked from the full schematic |

`ISET` accuracy follows the IC's specified `K_ISET` range and resistor tolerance;
0.242 A is a nominal design value, not a measured current. The selected 0.1 C
rate matches the Panasonic capacity-reference charge condition of 0.1 It for
16 hours. It does not by itself prove safe orbital charging.

## Launch-inhibit definition

- Three physically separate `SDS002RULC` switches shall each be held actuated by the
  deployer and shall independently interrupt enable/current paths while stowed.
- An accessible remove-before-flight device shall isolate the load side, and a
  second battery-service isolator shall break the battery/charge path during
  integration, following the BIRDS two-RBF functional split.
- Switch contacts are sensing/enable elements. The 100 mA contacts do not carry
  the spacecraft load current; MOSFET stages perform power interruption.
- Antenna deployment energy remains disabled until the OBC has observed release
  and an inhibit timer has expired. The delay value is a launch-ICD parameter.
- The exact two-jack/two-plug RBF selection, seven-stage positive-side topology,
  service connector and logical truth table are controlled by D1-FAB-001.
  PCB/layout, access geometry and removal procedure remain to be released.

## Known design issue: eclipse power cycling

The BQ25172 timer resets when its input or TS pin is toggled. A low-Earth-orbit
solar source disappears during every eclipse. On each restart the charger checks
the pack against its recharge threshold and may start a 20%-length intermittent
cycle. Before schematic release, a deterministic orbit-by-orbit state model must
bound cumulative charge at the worst battery temperature, state of charge and
spacecraft load. If this cannot be closed from manufacturer limits, representative
pack testing becomes a stated downstream boundary; the charger must not be
presented as flight-ready.

## Evidence limits

All selected semiconductors and switches are commercial parts. None is claimed
as radiation hardened or launch qualified. Circuit analysis, schematic review,
layout, environmental qualification and battery safety review remain open. The
present state is a traceable detailed-design input, not a tested EPS.
