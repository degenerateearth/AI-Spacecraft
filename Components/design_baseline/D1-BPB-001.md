# D1-BPB-001 — backplane and electrical interface control

Revision B · 2026-09-18  
State: **CUSTOM/TO BE VERIFIED — connector family and logical pin allocation frozen; PCB geometry not released**

## Scope

D1-BPB-001 is the passive interconnect between the combined OBC/EPS board, radio,
camera payload, front-access/safety board and antenna-release board. It is based
on the public BIRDS 50-pin stack interface but removes the configurable CPLD
crossbar and gives every retained signal a fixed function. This reduces software,
toolchain and latent-routing complexity.

## Slot population and allowed exceptions

| Slot | Board | Populated interfaces |
|---|---|---|
| J1 | D1-OBC/EPS-001 combined avionics | source/controller for both 3.3 V rails and switched buses; master for digital buses |
| J2 | D1-COM-001 | `+3V3_RF`, SPI, reset, `BUSY`, `DIO1`, and `RXEN`; pin 45 unconnected |
| J3 | D1-CAM-001 payload carrier | protected `+3V3_B` camera branch, UART, power enable and health analogs; no 5 V camera load |
| J4 | D1-FAB-001 front access/safety | inhibit/RBF sense, programming/service UART, battery/solar monitor test points; no mission load |
| J5 | D1-ANT-001 release/antenna board | deployment A/B, release continuity, `+3V3_B`; RF travels by a separate 50 Ω coaxial interface |

No RF signal is routed through the 50-pin connector. Exact coax, RF connector,
cable and bulkhead geometry belong to D1-COM-001/D1-ANT-001.

## Electrical rules

1. All digital pins are 3.3 V logic. A board requiring another logic level must
   contain its own named level translator.
2. Backplane connectors are rated at 1 A per contact by Hirose, but Degen-1 will
   derate power pins after thermal/vacuum analysis. No one contact is credited
   with the manufacturer maximum in the power budget.
3. The two contacts of each power net and its return shall use equal copper paths.
4. `+3V3_A` is the only always-on backplane rail. Other rails default off after
   reset and require explicit supervised enable.
5. Every off-board analog input requires series impedance and clamp analysis.
6. Each slot shall state its maximum inrush, steady and fault current before the
   protection component is selected.
7. Unused pins remain no-connect at the consuming board; they are not tied to
   ground unless this ICD assigns them as ground.

## Required release artifacts

1. Backplane KiCad schematic/PCB and exact connector placement from the BIRDS
   source geometry.
2. Slot-specific pin table with driver, receiver, direction, voltage limits,
   pull state, maximum current, reset state and fault response for every pin.
3. Connector mating-height and board-spacing stack drawing.
4. Copper-current, voltage-drop, grounding and return-path calculations.
5. Harness/connector schedule for solar, battery, RBF, deploy switches and RF.
6. Fabrication outputs, assembly notes, continuity/isolation acceptance test and
   mating-cycle control.

## Evidence limits and conflicts

The logical pin map is a project decision and has not been reviewed in schematic
form. The 90 × 86 mm board outline, 83 × 86 mm component envelope and 22.35 mm
payload allocation are source-backed BIRDS dimensions; their detailed keep-outs
must be transferred from the source drawing/CAD. Connector commercial ratings
do not establish vacuum, vibration, radiation or launch compatibility. Degen-1
must test the assembled interconnect and obtain launch-provider acceptance later.

## Revision B — radio control reconciliation

D1-COM-001 connects module pins 7 and 8 locally (TXEN/DIO2). Backplane
pin 45 is not connected to this net. No MCU output may drive it. The full
radio-carrier connector assignment is `D1-COM-001_pinmap.json`; logical
connectivity is defined, but digital power-off isolation remains unreleased.
