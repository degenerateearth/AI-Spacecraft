# DEGENERATE-1 known downstream dependencies

Revision 0.1 · 2026-09-15

`KNOWN DOWNSTREAM DEPENDENCY` means that final closure requires an external
event or evidence that is intentionally deferred during the present
public-information phase. It does not mean all related engineering work is
blocked. The readiness matrix identifies present work and retains `BLOCKED`
only where the specific activity itself cannot yet be performed credibly.

| ID | Dependency | Why it is downstream | Work required now | Trigger for engagement | Final closure evidence | Cost category |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| KDD-01 | Mission-specific launch opportunity, contract and controlled deployer/launch ICD | Public manuals support planning but cannot define the manifested mission | Maintain public EXOpod Nova mechanical baseline; screen alternates, orbit cases, loads and compliance margins parametrically | Before launch booking, final fabrication, qualification test tailoring or formal integration | Signed offer/contract, controlled ICD, mission orbit and accepted compliance matrix | Launch/integration |
| KDD-02 | As-procured supplier configuration, controlled ICDs, drawings and lot evidence | Public product pages do not define every revision, option or internal part | Complete public-data interface envelopes, tolerance ranges, derating, alternatives and procurement acceptance criteria | Before flight-component purchase or design release tied to a supplier option | Quote, order code, controlled data, certificate/lot records and incoming inspection | Spacecraft hardware |
| KDD-03 | Regulatory service determination, coordination and grants | Public rules permit planning; agencies and coordinators decide actual authorization | Prepare operator facts, proposed spectrum/imaging concept, draft data packages and compliance cross-reference | Before RF procurement that depends on the route, transmitting, remote-sensing operation or launch application | Applicable FCC/NOAA/FAA/ITU/IARU records and conditions | Regulatory/licensing |
| KDD-04 | Independent professional aerospace review | AI-generated analysis cannot provide independent approval | Build reproducible review packages, traceability, models and explicit questions | Before claiming Constitution Level 2 or releasing safety-critical/flight designs | Named reviewer qualifications, signed findings and dispositions | External professional/expert review |
| KDD-05 | Physical hardware and as-built measurements | CAD envelopes and catalog data do not establish fit or performance | Define inspection plans, fixtures, test points, tolerances and prototype strategy | Before engineering-model integration and flight procurement acceptance | Serial/lot-controlled inspection and test records | Spacecraft hardware; testing/verification |
| KDD-06 | Specialized environmental and deployer test facilities | Simulation cannot demonstrate launch survival, vacuum thermal behavior or dispenser compatibility | Define preliminary public-envelope test plans and analysis-to-test correlation requirements | After representative hardware exists and mission test tailoring is available | Approved procedures, calibrated raw data, anomaly dispositions and signed reports | Testing/verification; launch/integration |
| KDD-07 | Final fabrication and workmanship release | The current model contains unresolved geometry and interface assumptions | Mature editable CAD, drawings, schematics, materials/process controls and verification methods | After critical interfaces and release criteria close | Released fabrication package, traveler, inspection and configuration record | Spacecraft hardware; testing/verification |
| KDD-08 | Ground-station site and operating authority | Coverage, local approvals and RF exposure depend on the actual site/operator | Run parametric pass/link cases and define site-selection criteria | Before site construction, filing or end-to-end RF verification | Selected coordinates, equipment configuration, permissions and coverage validation | Ground station; regulatory/licensing |

These dependencies remain unknown or unperformed until their closure evidence is
in the repository. Deferral does not increase the project success level.
